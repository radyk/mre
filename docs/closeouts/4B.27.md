## 6. ITEM 1 -- NOT DONE, AND WHY

The later-direction counterfactual is NOT BUILT. Turn 1 above is unchanged: the
board still answers a "can I move this later" question with three ways to move
it earlier.

This is the largest item in the batch and the one with the most planner value,
and it is the one I did not get to. What it needs, having now read the ground it
would stand on:

  * `what-would-change` computes `est_*` floors and inverts them; there is no
    later direction anywhere in `counterfactual.py`.
  * The machinery to price a delay EXISTS and is the right machinery:
    `local_price.price_local` (4B.24) holds every other placement, moves the
    one bar, recomputes and REVALIDATES the ledger, and returns a refusal that
    already names the occupant with `holds_others`. The drags in section 4 are
    that path, driven by hand.
  * So the shape is: compute the target start (the maintenance day, or the next
    open window after it), price the pin locally, and report three things --
    what the delay costs, what it displaces, and whether the due date survives.
    The due-date verdict comes off the same service outcomes the card's
    affected list is built from.
  * The parse cost is a meaning widening on `what-would-change` (which today
    says "start earlier" in its own text), NOT a new intent.

WHAT MAKES IT MORE THAN AN AFTERNOON, and why I did not half-build it: on this
board a later drag is USUALLY A REFUSAL (both first attempts in section 4 were),
so the honest answer is dominated by the refusal branches -- calendar closure,
collision, chunked-op decline -- and each needs authored copy that says which
one happened and what it means. A version that only handled the priceable case
would answer the easy question and go quiet on the common one, which is the
failure mode this whole session is about.
