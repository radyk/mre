ITEM A2 -- THE FIX
======================================================================

`AnswerMemory` (interpreter.py) is the missing rung: the last DELIVERED answer's
route, question and RECORDS, per session, in process, bounded at 32 sessions.
Read-only derived state about our own output -- no write path into the canonical
model or the evidence store.

THE SEAM MATTERS AND IT IS NOT WHERE `remember_delivery` LIVES. That call sits
at the end of `dispatch`'s matched-route branch (5b), which a rolling route, a
tray answer, a synthesis answer and a CLARIFY never reach. The write is at
`run_ask`, after the dispatch returns, which is the ONE seam every live answer
passes through -- and a CLARIFY is precisely the specimen turn 8 needed.

  remembered:      every route, including CLARIFY and the honest floors
  NOT remembered:  prove-it itself, so a second drill-down still opens the
                   answer the planner is looking at rather than the drill-down
  cleared by:      forget_deliveries, which is now the ONE clear for
                   server-side conversation state

The last clause is 4B.16a's lesson taken seriously rather than repeated: that
errand found a RESET that cleared four channels and missed a fifth, and the
rider scolded across conversation boundaries for seven turns. Adding a sixth
store with its own separate clear would have reproduced it exactly.

THE THREE CASES ARE AUTHORED, AND THE FALL-THROUGH IS PRESERVED. The prior
answer is consulted ONLY on the branch that would otherwise have emitted the
no-target floor. A prove-it gesture that also names a real intent -- "but why",
one turn after a cause chain, the 4A.5a specimen -- is still answered as that
intent. What a planner wants there is the question answered, not our last
sentence re-opened. It is pinned by a test.

`PROVE_IT_NO_TARGET` was rewritten. After this ruling it is reachable only when
there is no prior answer at all, and its old second sentence then described a
turn that does not exist.

B3. INVARIANT VS MEASURED
----------------------------------------------------------------------

`order_disposition.census` now walks the canonical chain -- Demand ->
Fulfillment -> WorkPackage -> Operation -- and reports three counts.

MEASURED ON THE PINNED BOARD: 26 fully placed, 0 partly placed, 14 with none.
40 orders, 40 work packages, 40 fulfillments; every order has exactly one work
package. The 26 scheduled orders account for all 56 placed operations and the 14
beyond-horizon orders for the other 32 of the 88 declared.

IS IT INVARIANT? NO, AND THE ANSWER SAYS SO. `rolling_horizon._derive_maps`
builds `wp_of_demand[demand] = wp` -- one work package per demand, last
fulfillment wins. Every operation of an admitted package enters the window model
as a mandatory interval, so on a board where each order has one package,
all-or-nothing falls out. An order fulfilled by TWO packages has one of them
unreachable through that map: its operations are declared and never admitted,
and the order is split. Nothing enforces the one-package case.

So "no order on this board is split" is a fact about this schedule and "orders
are never partly placed" would be a claim about the product. The copy states the
measurement, then states the limit, and the limit clause is not optional. That
is 4B.21's ruling one level down: 4B.21 was about a count not naming its set;
this is about a fact not naming what KIND of fact it is.

The census deliberately reads ALL fulfillments rather than the solver's own
`wp_of_demand` map. Reading the solver's map would make a genuinely partial
order look complete by discarding the half it never admitted -- the guard would
be built out of the same blindness it exists to detect.

Three figures, `None` together when the chain cannot be walked. Three absent
figures are not three zeros.
