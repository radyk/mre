computed fact.

The ruling is transcribed in docs/04 (2026-07-30), amended in one respect with
its reason: it reaches copy that asserts WHETHER OR NOT it interpolates an

GATING WAS CONSIDERED AND REJECTED, with reasons recorded rather than assumed: a
board read per door is expensive on the near-miss path (two doors per refusal,
composed before any assembly) and it drifts -- a door added later without the
gate reintroduces the defect silently, whereas a rule that labels never assert
makes the class structurally impossible at zero runtime cost. The census found NO

  (1) SHAPE, general. `_WHY_PRESUPPOSITION`: a label of the form "why X IS /

3(c). NEAREST MATCHES, AND WHY THEY BEAT A LIST

Taken, with reasons. This is the correction path FOR A TYPO -- the overwhelmingly
likely state of the world is that the planner meant a machine that exists and
mistyped it. Ranking by similarity puts the answer they need first; an
alphabetical list puts it wherever the alphabet happens to put it, which on the
measured specimen was position 10 of 15, outside a cut of 8.

  explainer._nearest_names(typed, names, limit)
    - difflib.SequenceMatcher over case-folded names, ties broken
      alphabetically, so it is DETERMINISTIC (asserted 20x over a reversed
      input list)
    - a similarity FLOOR of 0.55: below it, NOTHING is offered. A guess dressed
      as a correction is the defect this route exists to avoid, and an honest
      count is a better answer than a wrong name.
    - the total travels either way -- (b) is kept, as the brief required.

  renderers._did_you_mean(names)
    one authored seam, so the machine and order branches cannot word the same
    correction two ways.
