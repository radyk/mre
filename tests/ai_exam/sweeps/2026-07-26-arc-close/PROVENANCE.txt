CLUSTERING METHOD (stated, not implied)
  Two synthesis answers are the same SHAPE when all three match:
    1. intent adjacency  -- the <=2 contracted intents the parse
                            judged closest, sorted
    2. subject kinds     -- order / machine / customer / concept,
                            sorted; NOT the refs
    3. dominant tool     -- the tool the loop called MOST (ties to
                            the earliest). The whole call set is
                            reported but is NOT part of the key:
                            one extra exploratory call would else
                            split a shape into two clusters of one.
  This is not semantic clustering. It SPLITS shapes whose parses
  disagreed, so it UNDER-states frequency; it never invents one.
  Two clusters with the same exemplar shape side by side is the
  tell, and a human merges them by naming the shape in a dossier.
